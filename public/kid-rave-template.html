What to Expect

Everything you'd want from a rave — designed from the ground up for kids.

DJ Sets

Kid-friendly EDM and chart remixes spun by real DJs. No algorithm. No playlist or pre-recorded sets, just genuine live performances.

LED Light Show

Full professional rig with moving heads, lasers, and color washes — calibrated to be thrilling and safe for young eyes.

Glow Gear

Glowsticks, glow bracelets, and accessories most of which are free of charge.

Sensory Quiet Zone

A low-stimulation area away from the main floor for kids who need a reset. No judgment, just space.

Photo Area

A dedicated backdrop and photo moment zone so you leave with memories that look as good as they felt.

Balloons

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before the drop.

Sound levels are kept under 85 dB throughout every event — the WHO recommended limit for children. A sound engineer monitors in real-time. We've never exceeded this limit.
Parents and guardians are required to stay — and most end up having the time of their lives. Kid Rave is a shared family experience. You're on the floor. You're in it.
We have a dedicated quiet zone away from the main floor. We also avoid rapid strobe sequences. Email us before the event if you have specific concerns and we'll make a plan together.
Tickets are non-refundable but transferable. Every person entering (including adults) needs a ticket. Children under 2 are always free.
Costumes and glow gear strongly encouraged! Light-up shoes, neon colors, glow bracelets, tutus, rave outfits — the more outrageous the better. Glow kits available at the door.
